Personal data processing policy

Version 1.0 dated 15 November 2026.

1. Operator and scope

Individual entrepreneur Veronika Evgenyevna Spiridonova (Taxpayer Identification Number, INN: 590616265164; Primary State Registration Number of an Individual Entrepreneur, OGRNIP: 326595800113213), referred to below as the Operator, processes the personal data of visitors to the Panika Production Agency website: https://panikaprod.github.io/.

Email for personal data enquiries: panika.production.agency@gmail.com.

This policy explains how enquiries submitted through the “start a project” and “join the team” forms are handled. It is not a services agreement and does not replace separate consent to personal data processing.

2. What information is used and why

2.1. Project enquiries

Purpose: to respond to an enquiry, contact the applicant, understand the brief, discuss whether the project can be carried out and prepare a proposal.

Data: name; company/brand, if provided; preferred contact; project type; approximate date; budget; description of the idea and any other information the visitor includes in the enquiry. Only fields marked with an asterisk in the form are required.

Legal basis: the visitor’s separate consent. If the interaction moves towards entering into or performing an agreement, the relevant independent legal grounds and documents apply.

Data is processed until the enquiry has been considered and project discussions have concluded, or until consent is withdrawn, whichever comes first. If the interaction continues on another lawful basis, including entering into or performing an agreement, data is processed only to the extent and for the period necessary for that basis.

2.2. Specialist applications

Purpose: to consider possible collaboration, include the specialist in a database of potential project contributors and contact them about suitable projects.

Data: name; profession; city; preferred contact; portfolio link; personal introduction and any other professional information provided voluntarily.

Legal basis: the specialist’s separate consent for this purpose. Submitting an application does not guarantee collaboration or employment.

Data is processed while the specialist remains in the database of potential project contributors, but not beyond the existence of this purpose or the withdrawal of consent, unless there is an independent legal basis for further processing. The Operator updates the application when informed of changes. If the database is discontinued, processing for this purpose ends.

2.3. Submission information

The application ID, date and time of receipt and form type are saved together with the completed fields. This information is used to keep track of enquiries, prevent duplicate records and monitor delivery of notifications to the Operator.

3. How data is processed

Data is provided by the visitor. Automated tools are used to receive and store applications, while review and communication are carried out manually. Processing operations include collection, recording, organisation, storage, updating, use, restriction of processing, deletion and destruction. Data may be transferred only to the extent and on the terms necessary for the relevant purpose and permitted by law.

These forms do not request passport details, health information, political or religious views or other special categories of personal data. Visitors are not identified using biometric data. Please do not include unnecessary confidential information or third-party personal data in free-text fields without an appropriate legal basis.

The Operator does not publish applications, contact details or enquiry content. Including a portfolio link in an application does not, by itself, constitute consent to publish the application on the website. Decisions about collaboration are not made automatically based on the application.

4. Services used and access

Google Apps Script and Google Sheets (Google LLC, USA) are used to receive and store applications. Telegram (Telegram Messenger Inc.) is used to deliver copies to the Operator. Processing uses these services’ international infrastructure, including infrastructure outside Russia. Public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; completed applications are not published in the website repository.

Information about how the services themselves process data: Google, Telegram, GitHub.

Access to applications is restricted to the Operator and persons lawfully granted access for the relevant task. Only the data necessary for that task should be made available. Confidentiality obligations are taken into account when granting contractors access.

5. Local browser storage

The selected language is stored in the browser until the selection is changed or local data is cleared. A form draft is stored locally on the device for up to one hour to prevent the entered text from being lost if the visitor accidentally leaves the page. Until the form is submitted, this draft is not sent to the spreadsheet or Telegram.

Visitors can clear local data through their browser settings. When using a shared device, please avoid leaving a completed form unattended.

Submitting a form does not constitute consent to advertising messages. If new analytics tools are introduced or the technologies used change, the Operator updates the processing information and requests separate consent where required.

6. Security and retention

Applications are protected through restricted spreadsheet access, HTTPS transmission, validation of submitted fields and storage of the Telegram bot token outside the website’s public code. The Operator restricts access to applications, protects their accounts and verifies requests to amend or delete data. Applications are not published in the website repository.

Processing periods are set out in section 2. When the purpose has been fulfilled or consent is withdrawn, the Operator stops processing and deletes the data, including working copies, unless there is an independent lawful basis for retaining it.

7. Rights and enquiries

Visitors may request information about the processing of their data, have it corrected, withdraw consent, request restrictions on processing or deletion in the cases provided by law, and contact Roskomnadzor or a court.

Requests should be sent to the email address in section 1. To help locate an application, please provide the name and contact used in the form. The Operator may request proportionate information to verify the applicant’s identity; unnecessary documents are not requested automatically. Requests are handled within the statutory time limits applicable to the type of request.

Withdrawing consent does not stop processing that may or must continue on an independent lawful basis, for example under an existing agreement. Where such a basis exists, the Operator explains it to the applicant.

8. Changes to this policy

The current approved version is published on the website with its date and version number. A material expansion of processing purposes is not introduced retroactively by changing this policy. New consent is requested where necessary.
